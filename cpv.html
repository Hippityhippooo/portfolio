Run discipline

  • One consolidated ask at Step 1, never again mid-run.
  • One arithmetic pass. Later sections cite, they never recompute.
  • Sub-skills read once. A second read is a defect.
  • Diagnostic text is written only when its check fires.
  • Steps 10e, 10h and 10k read existing results and select. Re-weighing evidence there is a defect.

The toy model

Every chart below runs on one invented company, ExampleCo. All inputs are assumptions chosen to exercise the engine, not a view on any stock. The toy works in EPS per share to keep the arithmetic visible; a real run builds revenue, then margins, then EPS. Move a control and every section recomputes through the same rules the skill states.

ExampleCo inputs (all assumed): base NTM EPS $4.00 · consensus NTM EPS $4.50 · hurdle multiple 18.0x from the growth table · C1 new product line $0.80 full size, 75% in window · C2 pricing reset $0.40 full size, 50% in window · RFR 4.2%, ERP 5.0% · bear scenario takes a 3% base-EPS haircut for operating leverage.

Steps 2–3 · Build · Catalysts and probability

A catalyst is only the increment above base.

Base carries the disclosed trajectory. A catalyst is only the increment above it, net of named offsets. Each catalyst is sized in full, cut to the share that lands inside the next twelve months, then multiplied by its hit probability. The stripped post-window slice is kept and returns at Step 9.

Steps 4–5 · Build · Four quarters, then shares and debt rolled

Revenue first, EPS on rolled shares, FCF last.

Strict order: revenue, gross margin for mix, opex split fixed and variable, EBITDA and EBIT, interest on the rolled net debt, tax, then EPS on rolled shares. Growth is charged its capex and working capital, so FCF falls out last. Where the company guides EPS, margins are backed out of the guide instead of assumed.

Next-twelve-month EPS by quarter, probability-weighted

Roll-forward identities

shares_t = shares_(t−1) + SBC − buyback$ / avg pricenetdebt_t = netdebt_(t−1) − FCF + buyback$ + dividend$ + acqBuybacks shrink shares and raise net debt in the same period. Average price is spot unless a disclosed plan price exists; a below-spot assumption is tagged A, assumed.
Convert consistencyDebt-form: face value in net debt, if-converted shares excluded from every denominator. Equity-form: the reverse, everywhere. A mixed basis counts the instrument twice and fails the completion checklist.

Step 6 · Price · Reconcile against consensus and the market

What the price implies, what consensus says, what we forecast. One driver explains the gap.X · Y · Z

Our forecast is reconciled twice. Against consensus, after matching horizon (FY vs NTM) and basis (GAAP vs non-GAAP, share count). Against the price, by backing the market-implied growth out of the current multiple. The output is one line: what the price implies (X), consensus (Y), our forecast (Z), the single driver D that explains Z minus X, and the event that makes D observable.

Price-implied vs consensus vs our forecast, NTM EPS

Gap to consensus, by tierT11

At the loud tier the engine assumes a slip first and re-checks five things in order: double-count, window, basis, staleness, timing. A loud gap that skipped the re-check is invalid at Step 10h.

Guards on market-implied growth6b

  • Conditionality. Implied growth comes from the same hurdle table, so it is never independent evidence for or against a discount rate drawn from that table.
  • Identifiability. Backing growth out of the multiple solves one free variable. It names a belief consistent with spot, not the belief.
  • Horizon. A P/E-implied growth is a perpetuity statement; build EPS is a one-year statement. A big implied gap beside a small one-year gap is horizon, not contradiction.
  • Estimate revisions. Read on the line the driver lives on. Alignment on another line is "adjacent" and caps conviction criterion 5 at half.revision vector
Steps 7–8 · Price · Valuation method and exit multiple

Settle the bridge first; the moat only moves the exit.

The bridge from enterprise value to equity is settled first, with one treatment across every valuation method and both periods. Then a decision tree picks the primary method, four valuation methods are run off the same forecast, and the spread between them tests bridge consistency. Moat and sector regime set how far the exit multiple may move.

Valuation method selection, leverage before capextree

Highlighted: the toy is profitable with no leverage, capex or D&A distortion, so P/E is primary. Lenders and insurers skip the EV methods entirely: P/E primary, P/B vs ROE as the cross-check. Both methods are computed when an input sits within 10% of a threshold.T50

Step 10d · Decide · Upside to downside

Upside to downside is the shape of the payoff, unweighted on purpose.asymmetry · 10d

The upside-to-downside ratio is the unweighted shape of the payoff at spot: upside to bull over downside to bear. It is bucketed unweighted on purpose so it stays independent of the upside to fair value. The engine then solves the spot prices at which the ratio crosses 1.5 and 0.8, which become the entry and trim levels the action menu cites.

Upside-to-downside ratio as spot moves (click the chart to move spot)

ratio = (bull_pv − spot) ÷ (spot − bear_pv)s*(k) = (bull_pv + k × bear_pv) ÷ (1 + k)Favorable, the entry level, at or below s*(1.5); exhausted, the trim level, at or above s*(0.8). The ratio rises as spot falls.
Degenerate casesSpot at or above bull PV: ratio 0, exhausted, no favorable band. Spot at or below bear PV: ratio undefined, treated as a staleness check on spot or the bear scenario. A negative-expected-return positioningsetup 6 re-solves the band as si(k) = (k × bull + bear) ÷ (1 + k) instead of relabelling.

Step 10k · Decide · Two ratings

Two ratings on two bases. Between them, a no-trade region.10k

New money asks whether new capital beats the cost of capital, so it is graded on the upside after discounting, through the positioning map. Existing holders ask whether the position expects an absolute gain, so they are graded on the return before discounting. The gap between the two bases is a no-trade region: hold what you own, buy none.
