The business

Brewed once, poured at the kerb

Coffee is cold-brewed in batches at one kitchen, carried in sealed jugs and poured over ice at the point of sale. Nothing is brewed on the cart, so a queue is never waiting on a machine. The cart is the shop: no lease, no receivables, and a weak site costs a relocation, not a lost fit-out. Two drinks, es kopi at Rp6,000 and kopi susu manis at Rp8,000 after a capped run of entry cups, paid in cash or by QRIS.

1 · KitchenBrew the day before

Cold brew in batches. One kitchen is planned for up to 1,200 cups a day.

2 · JugsLoad at dawn

Sealed jugs onto each cart in the morning, sized from the site's trailing seven-day median.

3 · CartRide to the site

An electric cart with a GPS tracker. It can work two or three sites in a day.

4 · KerbPour and settle

About ten seconds a cup. Every sale is paid on the spot.

Where it stands: eight locations and about 300 cups a day, which is roughly 37 cups per site. In the plan's own bands that is inside the "retain and analyse" range that starts at 34. Figures on the rest of this page are from the operating plan and investor deck of August 2026, revision C. They are model outputs, not forecasts, and the trading record replaces them as it comes in.

Where each Rp8,000 goes

Ten carts at 70 cups a cart-day. Of each Rp8,000 sold, Rp2,931 is net profit after every cost in the model, including depreciation and tax.

Volume sets what the fleet earns

Ten carts, 26 trading days, steady state from month 3. The two plan cases are 50 (conservative) and 70 (base). Margin rises with volume because the entry cup dilutes less and seller cost settles at 18 to 20% of revenue.

Monthly EBITDA by cups a cart-day

Operating leverage is low by design: about 45% of revenue is variable and under 3% is fixed cash cost, so a 10% move in revenue moves EBITDA by about 13%.

Reinvestment

Investors first, then the fleet

Investors receive the first Rp100m of operating cash. After that every rupiah goes into carts, up to ten new carts a month. A cart funded from one month's cash trades the next month at 60% of mature volume, then 85%, then full.

The structure works as designed. Only the strongest sites yield a pattern that can be repeated.

Every cart carries a GPS tracker. Sales are logged by hour, by cart and by seller, so a weak site and a weak seller can be told apart. No site is culled for good without trading under two sellers.

Five risks tested first

Site qualitySites will differ more than the model assumes. Each is reviewed on a schedule and weak ones replaced.
Site tenureA kerbside spot is used at the discretion of others. Carts are mobile and each route has backup sites.
Trading daysRain and holidays cut selling days. The model is re-based on days actually traded.
KitchenOne kitchen supplies every cart. Planned for 1,200 cups a day, with a second kitchen in the growth plan.
SellersA weak site and a weak seller look alike in the numbers. Sales are logged by seller as well as by site.
