Two minutes

Three rules, read first

Where a number came fromD G M Adisclosed, from guidance, modelled, assumed

Every material figure carries a tag: D disclosed, G from guidance, M modelled, A assumed, in that order of precedence. A figure that could have been disclosed or taken from guidance but was modelled is an error. The provenance log prints the share of the fair value that rests on modelled or assumed figures.

Who may type a numbercomposerThe report writer types no number

The engines do the arithmetic. The report writer re-expresses and types no number of its own: every printed number in an IC note is a reference such as {{S10.weighted_pv}} that the PDF layout resolves against the model output and stamps with its key.

What stops a rungatesA refused build is a correct outcome

Five thesis-break conditions, a completeness test on the model output, four checks that no untraced number reaches the note, sixteen plain-English rules on the prose, a date limit on every data row. When a test fails, the fix goes upstream of it; the test itself is never edited.

Governance

One rulebook, four skill typesflavors A–D

Four questions, asked in order. The first yes names the skill type, and the type decides which rules apply and which are banned.

1 · OperationalCWrites persistent state read again later?

Operational / tracking: energy-refresh, energy-explore.

2 · TransformDZero analytical authority, every figure traceable upstream?

Transform / render: gotrade-ic-note, stock-writeup-composer, stock-writeup-pdf, dhany-writeup-pdf, energy-macro-pack, human-voice.

3 · AnalyticalANumeric structure, same inputs same output?

Analytical / scoring: CPV, cpv-core, quick-stock-verdict, moat-analyzer, per-growth-checker, energy-read, energy-sector-analysis, energy-view, sector-tilt-book, dhany-writeup-composer.

4 · AdvisoryBPeople, motive, judgement?

Advisory / interpretive: weekly-market-note (editorial variant), situation-reflector, talk-progressor.

Each type is quarantined: analytical skills must not judge people or intent; advisory skills must not fake determinism; operational skills produce status only; transform skills introduce no number, rating or conclusion. Every threshold a skill introduces is [PROPOSED] until Faldo promotes it; no skill writes compliance text. A card with no type badge is a file that does not declare one in its first body line; the rulebook's registry says which type it expects, and the card says so.

One company, user-supplied data only, every module on every run. Version v4.10-lean, every constant in one registerT1–T52. The model output it emits is what the IC notes are built from.

Twelve steps in five stagesthe spine

Steps 2–5Build

Catalysts sized, dated and probability-weighted; the four-quarter build; shares and net debt rolled.

Steps 6–8Price

Reconciliation against consensus and against the market price; the multiple the valuation is anchored on; the exit-multiple assumption, bounded by moat quality and the sector regime.

Step 9Value

Bear, base and bull to one probability-weighted fair value; the sensitivity ranking (tornado); the breakeven solve.

Step 10Decide

Upside-to-downside ratio, conviction, thesis-break conditions, positioning, the two ratings.

Step 11EmitS1–S13 · SM · SP

The model output in thirteen sections, the completion checklist, the provenance log. No summary section.

Charging the same uncertainty twice (p shaded for a slip and the window cut for the same slip) is flagged by the enginedouble-conservatism and assigned to one field. p enters exactly two places: the build and the scenario weights.

The reconciliation against consensus and against the market pricestep 6 · twin reconcile is where a view that differs from the market is born: what the price implies (X), consensus (Y), our forecast (Z), the single driver that explains Z − X, and the event that makes it observable. Z near X means no difference from the market, and the run continues in confirmatory form.

Three scenarios, one probability-weighted fair valuearms · S10

GuardWhat it checks
Not just today's price carried forwardT47NTM metric × current multiple = spot × build ÷ consensus, a present value by identity. An exit value equal to it means the year-two roll-forward was dropped; the run is invalid and the fix is the roll-forward, never a lower discount rate.
The discount rate is a cost of equityCAPM DRr = RFR + β × ERP, written out, inside 7.5–20%T40. The sector × growth table is a hurdle-rate surface for backing growth out of a multiple; it never discounts a value.
Discounted and undiscounted agreeundiscounted identityweighted PV × (1 + DR) = weighted undiscounted, checked to rounding. The rating for new money reads the discounted value, the rating for existing holders the value before the cost of capitalentrant · holder.
Upside-to-downside bandasymmetry · 10ds*(k) = (bull + k × bear) ÷ (1 + k): favourable at or below the entry level s*(1.5), exhausted at or above the trim level s*(0.8). Bucketed on the unweighted ratio; the weighted ratio is stored as a cross-check only.

Conviction: five criteria graded 0, ½ or 110e · T32

CriterionGrading
1 · Upside to fair valueedgethe gap between price and fair value, after the cost of capital, either way: full above 12%, half 5–12%, zero at or below 5%T28b
2 · Spreadbear-to-bull spread over fair value: full below 35%, half 35–50%, zero above 50%T31
3 · Probability of the top catalystpfull at or above 70%, half 60–69%, zero below 60%T30
4 · Moat evidencefull when the proof-of-moat test is PASS, or PARTIAL with score ≥ 3.1 and direction not narrowing; half on PARTIAL with score ≥ 2.5; zero on FAIL, score below 2.5, or a genuine light read
5 · Estimate revisionsrevision vectorfull when supplied and aligned; half when flat or missing; zero when against

Half-credit ruleT35: a criterion failing purely on a missing input takes 0.5, at most two criteria; a third missing input makes the run limited by missing inputs and caps the conviction band at half. Two further limits: an assumed figure at the top of the sensitivity rankingT37 and a valuation-method shock at the top (proposed)T49. All three are stated even when they do not fire.

Dossier

The thirty-second verdictquick-stock-verdict

The thirty-second tier. One name in, one fixed card out, every judgment a threshold or a table lookup. Built for Sonnet at medium effort.

One search batch

Six calls fired in one parallel block, then at most one retry for a must-have slot (price, multiple or RSI); after that an unfilled slot reads n/a and the card ships. Price and market cap; forward P/E, consensus EPS and the sector median; the latest quarter, guidance and the reaction; catalysts and estimate revisions; gross margin, free cash flow and net debt; one live technicals page for RSI(14) and moving-average summaries.

Freshness checklist, all five, every run

1A price needs an as-of stamp from the current session or last close; no stamp, not a price.
2Banned sources for price, cap and RSI: undated pages, ratio explainers, screener metric pages, snippets older than two trading days.
3Cap cross-check: implied shares at or beyond 3% off the latest filing, recompute cap and mark it calc.
4A published P/E implying a price 3% off the verified one is recomputed and marked calc.
5Only a stale price found: the verdict is prefixed (STALE PRICE, with its date).

Valuation is sector-relative: forward P/E over the sector median, cheap below 0.85, expensive above 1.15, with one growth-adjusted shift at most. Driver overlayEDGE, with the veto still final: a present, dated driver and momentum not deteriorating promotes one step; no driver caps at MID; a negative driver demotes one step.
